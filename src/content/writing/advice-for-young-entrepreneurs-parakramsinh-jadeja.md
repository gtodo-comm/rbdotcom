<p><em>Main aisa youngsters ke liye bolna chahoonga, logon ko mann mein jo aata hai woh kare. </em>(I always say to youngsters, you should do what you are really passionate about.)</p>



<p>God has given everybody an equal opportunity. Stop listening to others, look inside yourself.</p>



<p>Take up one idea and start implementing it. You have nothing to lose.</p>



<p>Entrepreneurship does not just only mean starting a business. You can also work for a company with the mindset of an entrepreneur.</p>
