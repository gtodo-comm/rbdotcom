<p>Enthusiasm and passion are the only keys to success. An enthusiastic human being with good intent somehow manages to do good and land on the correct path.</p>



<p>When I started in my college, I didn’t know that a word called startup existed and till date I get the spelling of entrepreneurship wrong.</p>



<p>We all face problems but problems have a common pattern. A ‘big’ problem of the past becomes ordinary in the present. Similarly, the ‘big’ problem today will become ordinary issue. Just hang on!</p>



<p>I failed many times and faced many challenges. I am no superman but I just managed to stick with my goals. My failures taught me one thing – that I don’t have anything to lose from this point but only gain.</p>



<p>Perseverance matters the most. I love my failures and, in a true sense, made them my pillars of support.</p>



<p>Doing MBA from an obscure college, joining ordinary jobs and switching jobs are common traits among students today. You can do much much better! But first, you must believe in yourself.</p>



<p>Identify what you like and focus on that even while you are a student.</p>



<p>Your objective should not be, ‘I want to work for myself.’ Instead you should first work and if the work needs to be expanded, then you start a company.</p>



<p><em>Chahe kitne bhi </em>mistakes <em>karo. Log kuch bhi bole</em>, just keep doing it.</p>



<p>When you are enthusiastic about something or passionate about something, you somehow reach there.</p>
